Hex Cascade

A hexagon with 10 cells per side, 271 cells in all. Click a cell to fill it. Any empty cell touching 3 or more filled cells fills itself, and that can set off a chain.

Hover an empty cell to see what clicking it would set off (dashed outline). Click a filled cell you placed to clear it; anything that only filled because of it clears too. Auto-filled cells can't be clicked off directly.
