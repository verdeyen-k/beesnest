🐝 Bees Nest

Take turns filling cells of the hive. Any empty cell touching 3 or more filled cells fills itself, and that can set off a chain. Cells that fill themselves are yours if your click set them off.

Points

Your points = cells that filled themselves for you, minus cells you clicked. When the hive is full, whoever has the most points wins.

You can also scatter gray hexes at the start. They belong to nobody, but they count as filled, so they help set off chains.
